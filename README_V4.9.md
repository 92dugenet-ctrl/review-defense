# Review Defense V4.9 — Alert & Workflow Center

V4.9 centralizes operational alerts without performing external actions or changing policy automatically.

## Alert types
- MISSING_EVIDENCE
- HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED
- READY_FOR_APPROVAL
- SUBMISSION_TRACKING
- APPEAL_WINDOW
- URGENT_ESCALATION
- SLA_AT_RISK
- SLA_BREACH

Alerts are tenant-bound, auditable, role-controlled, and descriptive. CLIENT/VIEWER can view but cannot acknowledge or resolve alerts. OWNER/ADMIN/ANALYST can manage them.
