# Review Defense V4.10 — Analytics Center

V4.10 adds descriptive, tenant-scoped operational analytics: case volume, claims, evidence, coverage, policies, submissions, appeals, outcomes, analyst time, status breakdowns, coverage buckets and period comparisons.

Analytics are descriptive only. They do not rank political choices, predict Google decisions, estimate removal probability, or mutate policy configuration. All aggregates are organization-scoped.

## Validation
Run `pytest -q`: V4.1–V4.10 tests must pass.
