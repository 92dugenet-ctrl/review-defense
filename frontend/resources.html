Datasets

SAM 3D Artist Objects (SA-3DAO)

SA-3DAO is a dataset and benchmark of 1,000 image and artist-created 3D object pairs for evaluating real-world 3D reconstruction of object shape and layout. The dataset was introduced in our paper "SAM 3D: 3Dfy Anything in Images".

LuxRemix Dataset

The LuxRemix dataset is a synthetic dataset of 12K indoor scenes with per-light decomposition, which is designed for training models that decompose and re-mix indoor illumination from a single image.
